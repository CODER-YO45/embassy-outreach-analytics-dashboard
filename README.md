# Embassy Outreach & Diplomatic Engagement Analytics Dashboard

A professional, end-to-end data analytics and executive reporting model built in Microsoft Excel to track global diplomatic missions, monitor engagement performance, and enforce data governance.

---

## 📊 Project Overview
This project manages an operational tracking dataset of **15 global diplomatic missions** across **6 regions** (Africa, Asia-Pacific, Europe, Latin America, Middle East, and North America). It transforms raw outreach logs into an interactive, executive-ready Business Intelligence dashboard equipped with dynamic visual elements and data governance audits.

---

## 🗂️ Workbook Architecture & Structure
The workbook is organized into a clean, presentation-ready structure:

1. **`Project_Summary`**: Executive briefing cover page outlining project objectives, key metrics, data insights, and AI collaboration notes.
2. **`Database`**: Cleaned and standardized raw data source tracking 15 embassies, regions, contact dates, engagement scores, and follow-up statuses.
3. **`Summary_View`**: Backend analytical engine powered by PivotTables tracking active conversion rates (`53.3%`), regional distribution, and average follow-up delays.
4. **`Data Quality Review`**: Formal data hygiene and risk audit log documenting exception handling (missing contact/follow-up dates for Australia, Italy, and Argentina) and verification checks.
5. **`Dashboard`**: Executive UI featuring 5 dynamic KPI summary cards, regional participation bar charts, status donut charts, and **synchronized global Slicers**.

---

## 🚀 Key Analytical Findings & Metrics
* **Total Universe:** 15 active diplomatic missions monitored.
* **Conversion Rate:** Overall active conversion rate stands at **53.3%** (8 active, 5 pending, 2 closed).
* **Geographic Concentration:** Europe and Asia-Pacific represent the highest volume of diplomatic outreach (9 out of 15 missions).
* **Data Hygiene:** 100% of foreign mission titles standardized; missing timestamp anomalies logged and flagged for administrative follow-up.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Demonstrated
* **Microsoft Excel:** Advanced PivotTables, Power Query text transformations, formula calculations (`IF`, lookup logic), conditional formatting, and multi-chart Report Connections for Slicers.
